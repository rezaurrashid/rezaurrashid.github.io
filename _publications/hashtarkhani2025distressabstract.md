This study uses explainable machine learning to characterize baseline psychosocial distress before radiation therapy. An XGBoost model combined with SHAP-based interpretation was used to examine clinical, demographic, behavioral, and socioeconomic contributors to distress. Younger age, lung cancer diagnosis, metastatic disease, current smoking, dual Medicare-Medicaid coverage, and divorced marital status emerged among the strongest contributors to higher predicted distress, supporting the potential use of interpretable models to inform pre-treatment supportive care.
