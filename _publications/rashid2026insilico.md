This study evaluates AI-based cancer patient navigation in a preclinical simulation environment using synthetic patient personas and multi-turn radiotherapy-support dialogues. Human reviewers assessed whether the AI navigator recognized patient concerns, communicated clearly and respectfully, provided useful guidance, and responded appropriately to clinical and social barriers. The work provides an in silico-human evaluation framework for stress-testing patient-facing AI before prospective clinical deployment.
