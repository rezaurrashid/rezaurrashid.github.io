This retrospective cohort study integrated baseline patient-reported psychosocial distress measures with clinical and census tract-level socioeconomic data from 1,953 patients receiving external beam radiation therapy. Negative binomial regression and XGBoost identified higher distress, learning or communication barriers, and genitourinary malignancy among factors associated with more missed treatment days. The findings support incorporating psychosocial assessment alongside clinical and socioeconomic information when evaluating radiation treatment interruption risk.
