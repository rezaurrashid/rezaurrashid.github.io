This work presents a literature-calibrated digital-twin framework for simulating longitudinal radiation oncology trajectories and radiation therapy interruptions. Synthetic patient profiles integrate clinical, treatment, and social determinants of health characteristics with rule-based longitudinal changes in factors such as functional status, fatigue, distress, treatment burden, and access barriers. The framework is designed to support controlled in silico evaluation of interruption risk and patient-navigation strategies when prospective experimentation is impractical.
