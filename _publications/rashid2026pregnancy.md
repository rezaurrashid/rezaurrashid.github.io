This study evaluated 10 commercial, open-weight, and encoder-based sentiment classification models using 15,619 pregnancy-related Reddit posts and comments, with an expert-adjudicated reference set of 200 posts. On the fixed held-out test set, Gemini-3-Flash achieved the highest observed weighted F1-score (0.662), although confidence intervals overlapped among leading models. Human annotator disagreement and differences between posts and comments highlighted the limitations of treating broad sentiment labels as stand-alone measures of maternal-health experiences.
