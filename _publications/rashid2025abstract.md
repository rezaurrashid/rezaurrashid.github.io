Adherence to scheduled radiation therapy is an important determinant of treatment quality and outcomes. This study developed interpretable machine-learning models to identify patients at elevated risk of multiple unplanned radiation therapy interruptions and to examine potentially modifiable contributors to that risk. Clinical, socioeconomic, demographic, behavioral, and treatment-related features were evaluated using predictive modeling, SHAP-based interpretation, and supplementary structural analysis, highlighting treatment intensity, insurance, social vulnerability, marital status, and travel distance as important factors associated with interruption risk.
