I am a **Postdoctoral Research Fellow** at the [Center for Biomedical Informatics (CBMI)](https://uthsc.edu/cbmi), Department of Pediatrics, College of Medicine, at the *University of Tennessee Health Science Center (UTHSC)*, working with [**Dr. Arash Shaban-Nejad**](https://uthsc.edu/cbmi/about/faculty-staff.php).

My research focuses on developing **explainable, causal, and trustworthy AI methods** for complex data-driven problems, with a major emphasis on healthcare and cancer research. My current work spans explainable prediction of radiation therapy interruptions, multimodal clinical AI and digital twins, LLM-based patient simulation and navigation, health-related language analysis, and large-scale clinical data analytics. I am particularly interested in methods that move beyond prediction toward understanding why outcomes occur and how computational models can better support decision-making.

I earned my **Ph.D. in Computer Science** from the *University of North Carolina at Charlotte* in 2024, where I worked with [**Dr. Gabriel Terejanu**](https://cci.charlotte.edu/directory/gabriel-terejanu/) in the [Uncertainty Quantification Lab (UQLab)](https://www.uncertaintyquantification.org/). My doctoral research focused on probabilistic causal structure learning, causal feature selection, and graph neural networks. I received my **B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering** from *BRAC University* in 2017.
